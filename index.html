Student Workshop on Assured Autonomy for National Security

November 16-17, 2026  |  Engineering Center, University of Colorado Boulder

Hosted by CU Boulder and the Front Range Consortium

Student registration Draft agenda

The workshop

  • A panel of defense leaders from NORAD and U.S. Northern Command and U.S. Space Command on the morning of November 16: how their commands are adopting AI and autonomy, where the gaps are, and what they need from the next generation of engineers.
  • Students from across the Front Range Consortium member schools, with travel and lodging covered for those coming from outside Boulder.
  • Hands-on work with real robot platforms in a CU Boulder robotics lab.

The panel sets the charge for the two days. On day two, student teams work against that charge alongside engineers from industry and government.

Who should come

Students

Undergraduate and graduate students at Front Range Consortium member universities. Space is limited to 30 students, first come first served. There is no registration fee, meals are provided, and travel and lodging are covered for anyone coming from outside Boulder.

Register here

Government and industry

Senior guests are welcome for the Monday morning panel and breakfast. Day two is the best fit for engineers who want to work alongside the student teams and bring the operational view: what the mission needs and which constraints apply.

Please contact Christoffer Heckman.

Logistics

Venue: ECCS 1B21, Engineering Center, University of Colorado Boulder. The workshop room sits next to the robotics lab. Parking and wayfinding details will follow with the final agenda.

Meals: the Front Range Consortium provides breakfast and lunch both days.

Hosts and contact

Christoffer Heckman, Professor of Computer Science, University of Colorado Boulder, leads the workshop. Questions: christoffer.heckman@colorado.edu

The Front Range Consortium is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that unites Colorado universities, defense organizations and industry partners for national security research, education and workforce development. Director: Paul Huffman. frontrangeco.org
